The house

We built this place for downtime, and winter is when it does that best. Turn on the gas fireplace, cook something slow in a kitchen that has what you need, and pad around three full baths with radiant-heat floors. Upstairs, two primary bedrooms with blackout blinds and beds by Hästens and Restoration Hardware. It snows, it gets very quiet, and the woods outside the windows go blue at four o'clock.

Downstairs is one open floor: a dining area by the fireplace, a long living room with a Frame TV, board games and puzzles, and between them a well-stocked kitchen with an island. When you want air, Minnewaska and Mohonk are twenty minutes away, and there's a sauna on the Hudson in Kingston with a cold plunge in the river.

It's a different house in every season.

Winter

Snow on the trees, the fireplace on, and staying in as the whole plan.

Spring

The gardens wake up. Raised beds of herbs, greens and flowers.

Summer

Fireflies fill the woods behind the yard. Hence the name.

Fall

Farm stands, orchards and harvest season, all within fifteen minutes.

Outside

A fenced yard that opens into the woods

The whole yard is deer-fenced. That keeps the gardens intact, keeps ticks down, and gives kids and small dogs room to run.

There's Gloster teak furniture on the patio, a Weber gas grill, a Solo Stove Yukon fire pit under string lights, a hammock between two cedars, and enough lawn for frisbee. The screened porch off the kitchen is where most dinners end up.

Good to know

Before you book

Check-in / out
In after 3:00 PM, out by 11:00 AM. Self check-in with a keypad.
Early or late
Early check-in and late checkout are possible case by case. It depends on whether another guest arrives or leaves that day, so just ask.
Pets
Tell us about any pet before booking. Dogs case by case; smaller, well-trained dogs fit best. No cats (severe allergies).
Quiet house
No parties, and no groups planning to be loud late at night. Guest and pet count should match your booking.
Fewer ticks
Ticks ride in on deer, and the fence keeps the deer out. You'll see far fewer here than on an unfenced property in the area. A quick check after time outside in late spring and early fall is still smart.
Access
The whole house is yours except the basement, which is storage and utilities.
Work
Spectrum internet at 163 Mbps and a dedicated desk in Bedroom 2.
Laundry
Washer and dryer in the house.
